03

SEC EDGAR institutional-flow tracker

PYTHON · IN DEVELOPMENT

A tool that reads institutional filings from SEC EDGAR and reconstructs position flow: which filers accumulated or distributed a security, at what scale relative to their book, and over what period. It separates 13F holdings, which are filed up to 45 days after quarter end, from Form 4 insider transactions, which are filed within two business days.
