The idea

The platform is the product.
Strategies are pluggable.

Most self-built trading systems are a strategy with plumbing attached — when the strategy stops working, the system dies with it. Profit Pilot inverts that. The platform is permanent infrastructure whose job is to supervise, constrain, measure and retire the strategies running inside it. A sleeve that stops earning its risk budget gets throttled by the allocator and eventually refused promotion. The system is designed to outlive any idea in it.
