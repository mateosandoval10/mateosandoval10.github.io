Thesis

A swing-rotation score built from two components: money-flow strength (20-day Chaikin-style CMF, 65% weight) and volatility calm (35% weight), each expressed as a 1-year percentile rank so the score is self-normalizing. Buy TQQQ when the composite crosses above 70, sell when it crosses below 30. v1 of this system worked (+464% over 15 years) but carried an ugly left tail — v2 exists because of a trade-by-trade loss autopsy, shown below with the receipts.

The loss autopsy that produced v2

All twelve v1 losers shared three patterns:

1 · Losers die fast

Every loser was held 11–109 days; every big winner 61–462 days. The signal detects trends well — its failures happen quickly.

2 · Crashes outrun the exit

The worst losses (−33% Feb 2025, −28% Dec 2022, −19% Volmageddon 2018) were fast crashes: the score exit needs weeks of bad data to fall below 30, and a 3x ETF is down 30% by then.

3 · It buys complacency tops

Rewarding low volatility means entries cluster right before volatility expansions — Jan 2018 and Feb 2025 were both buys within days of a melt-up high.

The v2 changes

A 200-day SMA entry filter (kills bear-rally buys), a volatility-spike emergency exit (vol %-rank crossing 85), and a 25% disaster stop. A 20% stop tested worse — the Aug 2019 and Brexit dips bottom at −21/−23% and whipsaw it out of eventual winners.

Net +$46,425 on $10,000 (+464%). Ten winners, twelve losers — the system is right less than half the time and profitable anyway, because winners are held ~5x longer than losers. Under v2's filters, trade 15 (the Dec 2022 bear-rally buy) never happens: the 200-SMA gate blocks it.

v2 rule changes (shipped configuration)

useTrend  → entries require close > SMA(200)        // kills bear-market rally buys
useVolX   → exit when vol %-rank crosses above 85    // crash exit faster than the score
useStop   → hard stop 25% below entry                // caps the left tail
// tested and rejected: 20% stop (whipsaws out of -21/-23% dips that recover),
// vol-exit at 80 (fires on noise, degrades PF vs the 85 setting)
Limitations. 22 trades is a small sample; treat every statistic accordingly. Single instrument, in-sample, and the improvement was designed after inspecting these very losses — the v2 deltas need out-of-sample confirmation before being trusted. The honest claim is narrower: the risk caps bound the left tail by construction, whatever the future signal quality turns out to be.
