The rejection log

Roughly thirty candidate improvements have been tested and rejected across these systems and the intraday research program. Each strategy page carries its own rejected experiments with measured numbers; four findings shaped everything else here.

Reaction speed usually loses

A faster de-risking variant of VTTE more than doubled turnover, sold volatility spikes at their lows, and made the maximum drawdown worse (41.9% → 49.6%) while cutting net return by nearly half. Slow rebalancing is a feature, not laziness.

In a reversion system, the target is the edge

Replacing CRS's channel-midline target with a trailing stop kept net return identical but dropped the profit factor from 2.28 to 1.79 and the win rate from 64% to 47%. The reversion to the middle is the reliable move; everything after it is noise you pay for.

Sizing is what turns a signal into a system

CBS's identical signal stack sized all-in per trade produced a bigger headline (+1,040%) and an 89% drawdown. Fixed-fractional risk sized off the stop cut the largest loss from $4,720 to $1,366 and raised the profit factor from 2.87 to 3.16 — on exactly the same signals.

A big backtest predicts almost nothing

Measured directly on 60,480 configurations of the intraday research engine: the correlation between train-period net and out-of-sample net is +0.087, and the top 100 configs by backtest lost money out of sample 79% of the time. The experiment is on the methodology page.

What these results are not

In-sample
The parameters were chosen with these histories visible. Split-sample and slippage stress reduce selection effects; they do not eliminate them
Single-history
One symbol per system, one market era. The cross-symbol tests share that era, so they test parameter robustness, not regime robustness
Small samples
22 to 69 trades per system. Every statistic derived from them carries a correspondingly wide error bar, whether or not that bar is printed
Upper bounds
Real execution is worse than modeled execution, and the modeling here is a simulation of a market, not the market
Next gate
Live paper trading, before any of this becomes a capital conclusion

Remaining work: subperiod splits for VTTE and CRS (currently blocked by plan-level deep-backtesting limits), a multi-name universe test rather than hand-picked symbols, and parameter-perturbation Monte Carlo. The methodology page shows the standard I am working toward — the intraday research pipeline applies placebo tests and bootstrap intervals that these Pine Script backtests do not yet have.
