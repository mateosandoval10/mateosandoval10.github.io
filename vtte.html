Thesis

Leveraged index ETFs compound spectacularly in calm uptrends and destroy capital in volatile downtrends — volatility decay on a 3x product is quadratic in realized vol. So don't predict anything. Gate the holding to the uptrend regime, and let realized volatility itself set the position size. This is the same architecture as institutional vol-control indices, expressed in ~60 lines of Pine.

There is no entry signal to overfit. The system holds one decision variable: how much exposure does current volatility justify?

The honest comparison: raw buy-and-hold TQQQ earns more — if the holder survives two >80% drawdowns without capitulating, which essentially nobody does. VTTE keeps most of the compounding at roughly half the worst drawdown, and its exposure drops mechanically before crises mature because volatility clusters. Against SPX, the engine compounds at roughly double the rate.

Failed experiment (kept in the record)

REJECTED — fast de-risking variant. Replaced the 20-day vol estimate with max(5-day, 20-day) and allowed de-risking any day of the week ("fast to cut, slow to add"). Result: turnover more than doubled (155 → 376 events), the system repeatedly sold volatility spikes at their lows and re-bought higher a week later. Net return fell from +3,862% to +2,158%, profit factor 3.33 → 1.98, and max drawdown got worse (41.9% → 49.6%). Slow rebalancing is a feature: reaction speed costs more than it saves.

Source

//@version=6
strategy("Vol-Target Trend Engine", shorttitle="VTTE", overlay=true,
     initial_capital=10000, commission_type=strategy.commission.percent,
     commission_value=0.05, slippage=1, pyramiding=500,
     process_orders_on_close=true, calc_on_every_tick=false)

volLen    = input.int(20,    "Realized vol lookback (days)",  minval=5)
targetVol = input.float(45.0,"Target annualized vol (%)",     minval=5,  step=1.0)
smaLen    = input.int(200,   "Regime SMA length",             minval=50)
band      = input.float(10.0,"Rebalance band (% of target)",  minval=1,  step=1.0)
fastDrisk = input.bool(true, "Immediate de-risk at 1.5x over target")

retD    = close / close[1] - 1.0
realVol = ta.stdev(retD, volLen) * math.sqrt(252) * 100.0
smaReg  = ta.sma(close, smaLen)
regime  = close > smaReg

frac      = math.min(1.0, targetVol / math.max(realVol, 1.0))
targetQty = regime ? strategy.equity * frac / close : 0.0
posQty    = strategy.position_size
delta     = targetQty - posQty

weekTurn   = timeframe.change("1W")
regimeFlip = regime != regime[1]
overExp    = fastDrisk and posQty > 0 and targetQty > 0 and posQty > targetQty * 1.5

if weekTurn or regimeFlip or overExp
    if not regime and posQty > 0
        strategy.close_all(comment="regime off")
    else if regime and math.abs(delta) > targetQty * band / 100.0
        if delta > 0
            strategy.order("Add",  strategy.long,  qty=delta,  comment="scale up")
        else
            strategy.order("Trim", strategy.short, qty=-delta, comment="de-risk")

Full script (with regime visuals and live status table) available on request and in the published TradingView library.

Limitations. Single instrument, single history, in-sample. TQQQ's own listed history begins in 2010 — a mostly secular-bull window; the regime gate is what carries the 2020 and 2022 stress periods, but the configuration has not been split-sample validated yet (subperiod windowing for this engine is queued behind plan-level deep-backtesting limits; the CBS page shows the template this system will follow).
